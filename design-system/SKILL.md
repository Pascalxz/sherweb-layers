## Quick brand reminders
- Primary accent: **Sherweb Red `#DB4227`** (used on CTAs, emphasis, underlines). Do NOT overuse — one red element per section at most.
- Deep navy (`#072A4A`) for headings on light; sky blue (`#DFF0FF`) for soft section backgrounds; bright blue (`#0A96ED`) for links and secondary accents.
- Typography: **Montserrat** everywhere. Headings 700–800, uppercase eyebrows with ~1px letter-spacing, body 400–500.
- Layout: generous white space, soft blue section bands, full-width band cards with 16–24px radii, light borders rather than heavy shadows.
- Iconography: **Font Awesome 6** (solid for most UI; brands for social). Avoid emoji.
- Tone: professional-warm, partner-first, benefit-led. Title-case headlines, sentence-case body. Bilingual EN/FR in many contexts.
