Brand promise — 2026 campaign principle

ExpectBetter2026 voice

The artifact is the brand.

The campaign itself should embody Expect Better. Email and questionnaire feel like one continuous reflection — not a marketing artifact wrapping a measurement instrument.

This is the first survey I've ever received that doesn't feel like a survey.

Voice attributes — always-on

Professional
Expert, precise, never casual or hype-y. We respect the reader's time and IQ.
"Microsoft's partner program has changed. Keep earning incentives."
Neutral
Factual, low adjective-density. No hype, no fear, no flattery. Let the value speak.
"Acronis Cyber Protect — backup and security in one console."
Confident
We make claims and stand by them. No hedging ("might", "could help you"), no apology.
"You'll grow margin on every Microsoft seat."
Supportive
Partner-first. The reader is the hero; we're the guide. "You" + "we", never "users".
"You bring the client relationship. We handle the licensing."
